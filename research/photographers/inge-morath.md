# Inge Morath (1923–2002)

Inge Morath (full name: Ingeborg Hermine Morath) was an Austrian-born photojournalist and portraitist, a full member of Magnum Photos from 1955. The ICP archive page (fetched 2026-05-22) records her birth year as "1923," birthplace as "Graz," and death year as "2002" (src-icp-inge-morath-archive). Wikipedia (fetched 2026-05-22) provides day-month resolution: born "27 May 1923" in "Graz, Austrian Republic"; died "30 January 2002" in "Manhattan, New York, U.S." (src-wikipedia-inge-morath-pointer). Magnum Photos (fetched 2026-05-22) independently corroborates: born "1923" in "Graz, Austria"; died "January 30, 2002" in "New York City" (src-magnum-inge-morath). Two-source (Magnum + Wikipedia) corroboration of the January 30, 2002 / New York death tokens promotes those above pointer-only status.

ICP records her nationality as "American (b. Austria)"; Magnum gives "Austrian/American"; Wikipedia gives "Austrian." The dual-nationality framing reflects her immigration to the United States in 1962 (per ICP: "She moved to the United States in 1962, and married the playwright Arthur Miller" — src-icp-inge-morath-archive).

## Career

ICP (fetched 2026-05-22) provides the fullest narrative of Morath's early career. Verbatim from the ICP archive (src-icp-inge-morath-archive): "Inge Morath was born in Graz, and educated in France and Germany. She studied languages at the Universities of Berlin and Bucharest, and worked as an editorial translator and interpreter for the U.S. Information Service Publications in Salzburg, and then as a radio writer and as literary editor for Vienna's *Der Optimist* magazine. In 1952, she apprenticed in London to Simon Guttmann, a major figure in photojournalism and one of the founders of *Berliner Illustrierte Zeitung*; in 1954 Morath joined Magnum, the cooperative photo agency founded in 1947 by Henri Cartier-Bresson, Robert Capa, George Rodger, and Chim (David Seymour). She was assistant to Cartier-Bresson in 1953-54."

**MAGNUM JOINING YEAR DISCREPANCY:** ICP gives "in 1954 Morath joined Magnum" (src-icp-inge-morath-archive). The Magnum Photos profile (fetched 2026-05-22) gives 1953 as the year she joined as photographer and 1955 as the year she became full member (src-magnum-inge-morath). Wikipedia (fetched 2026-05-22) gives 1953 (as editor) / 1955 (as full photographer member) (src-wikipedia-inge-morath-pointer). The ICP's "1954" reading may reflect the date of her formal photographer membership after the 1953-54 Cartier-Bresson assistantship. The discrepancy is noted and not adjudicated here.

ICP continues (verbatim from src-icp-inge-morath-archive): "More than a dozen books resulted from her travels in the following thirty years, including *Fiesta in Pamplona* (1954), *Tunisia* (1961), *In Russia* (1969), and *Chinese Encounters* (1979), and her warm and intimate portraits were reproduced in such magazines as *LIFE*, *Paris-Match*, *Saturday Evening Post*, and *Holiday*." Her marriage to playwright Arthur Miller in 1962 is recorded verbatim by ICP. Two monographs appeared: *Inge Morath* (1975) and *Portraits by Inge Morath* (1986); a retrospective toured Europe in 1993-94 (src-icp-inge-morath-archive).

The Magnum profile notes she "assisted Henri Cartier-Bresson during 1953-54" (src-magnum-inge-morath), consistent with ICP's "She was assistant to Cartier-Bresson in 1953-54."
