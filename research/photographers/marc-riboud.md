# Marc Riboud (1923–2016)

Marc Riboud was a French photojournalist and long-standing Magnum Photos member, best known for his extensive documentation of Asia and his iconic 1967 anti-war photograph "The Ultimate Confrontation: The Flower and the Bayonet." The ICP archive page (fetched 2026-05-22) records his birth year as "1923," death year as "2016," and nationality as "French" (src-icp-marc-riboud-archive). Wikipedia (fetched 2026-05-22) provides day-month resolution: born "24 June 1923" in "Saint-Genis-Laval, France"; died "30 August 2016" in "Paris, France" (src-wikipedia-marc-riboud-pointer). The Magnum Photos profile (fetched 2026-05-22) independently corroborates: born "1923" in "Lyons" (Lyon); died "August 30, 2016" in "Paris" (src-magnum-marc-riboud). Two-source (Magnum + Wikipedia) corroboration of August 30, 2016 / Paris death tokens promotes those above pointer-only status.

**BIRTHPLACE NOTE:** Magnum gives "Lyons" (the older English spelling of Lyon). Wikipedia gives "Saint-Genis-Laval" — a commune in the Lyon metropolitan area (Métropole de Lyon). ICP does not name the birthplace. Neither reading contradicts the other; Saint-Genis-Laval is within the Lyon area. The discrepancy is noted and not adjudicated here.

## Career

ICP (fetched 2026-05-22) provides the primary narrative of Riboud's career trajectory. Verbatim from the ICP archive (src-icp-marc-riboud-archive): "Marc Riboud became interested in photography in 1937, when he took his first photographs with his father's Vest Pocket Kodak camera. It was not until 1953, however, that he became a full-time photographer. During the intervening years, Riboud studied engineering and fought for the French Resistance in World War II, earning the Croix de Guerre in 1945. After meeting Henri Cartier-Bresson, Robert Capa, and Chim (David Seymour) in Paris in 1951, he left his job as a research engineer to make a living through photography. He worked free-lance until in 1953 he was invited to join Magnum, the co-operative photographic agency founded by Cartier-Bresson, Capa, Chim, and George Rodger."

The Magnum profile (fetched 2026-05-22) adds that he was "Invited to join Magnum as an associate by Henri Cartier-Bresson and Robert Capa in 1953" and became a full member in 1955 (src-magnum-marc-riboud). Wikipedia (fetched 2026-05-22) gives "By 1953 he was a member of the organization" (src-wikipedia-marc-riboud-pointer), consistent with ICP and Magnum.

ICP continues (verbatim from src-icp-marc-riboud-archive): "Through Magnum, he traveled to Asia and Eastern Europe several times and gained recognition as a specialist in documenting Asian cultures. Riboud was a very active member of Magnum, in terms of his photographic production and his official capacity, as vice-president, president, and chairman of the board. Riboud retired in 1979, but the agency maintains his archives." Wikipedia records his 1967 photograph "The Ultimate Confrontation: The Flower and the Bayonet" as "among the most celebrated anti-war pictures" (src-wikipedia-marc-riboud-pointer).
