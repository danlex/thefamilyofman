## Provenance

The MoMA Master Checklist (`src-moma-exh-0569-master-checklist`, Tier-1, in-repo, read this session) records plate #409 as: *"China — Henri Cartier-Bresson, Magnum, French — 18 x 27 1/4 cm"*. The checklist records no title, date, or subject description. The print dimensions (18 × 27 1/4 cm) make it a wide-format landscape print — larger in the horizontal dimension than Cartier-Bresson's other eight plates in the exhibition, most of which are in the 18 × 12 cm portrait range.

This is the eighth of eleven Henri Cartier-Bresson plates in the checklist, and the only one set in China. The preceding seven plates span France (×3: #31, #259, #366), Indonesia (×2: #128, #190 Bali), USA (×1: #288), and Kashmir (×1: #371); a ninth plate follows at #442 (Iran), then #481 (Java) and #483 (Spain). The geographic breadth of Cartier-Bresson's plates across the exhibition is documentable from the checklist itself.
