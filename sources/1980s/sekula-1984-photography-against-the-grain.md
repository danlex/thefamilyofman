## Citation

Sekula, Allan. *Photography Against the Grain: Essays and Photo Works, 1973–1983*. Halifax: Nova Scotia College of Art and Design Press, 1984.

Reprinted: Rochester, NY: Visual Studies Workshop Press, 1984.

## Relevance

The collected essays that bring together Sekula's foundational materialist critique of photography as ideology, including earlier versions and companion texts to "The Traffic in Photographs" (1981, `src-sekula-1981`). This collection is the canonical book-form gathering of Sekula's most influential theoretical essays from the decade 1973–1983. It constitutes the single most important institutional presentation of the Marxist-materialist critique of photography's ideological function — the theoretical position from which *The Family of Man*'s claims to universal humanism were most systematically challenged in the period.

The NSCAD Press (Nova Scotia College of Art and Design) is an art-school academic press; Sekula is named in `CREDIBILITY.md` as a "critical theory of record" author at Tier 2.
