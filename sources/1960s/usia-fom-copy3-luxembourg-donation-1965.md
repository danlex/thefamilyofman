## Relevance

The USIA world tour of *The Family of Man* operated through multiple copies of the exhibition circulating simultaneously. Copy 3 (second European tour) circulated 1957–1965. At Steichen's request, Copy 3 was not dispersed at the end of the tour but was instead presented to the Government of Luxembourg for permanent display. This marks the conclusion of the USIA touring phase and the beginning of the exhibition's permanent installation history in Luxembourg. The physical object that now resides at Clervaux Castle is derived from this transfer.
