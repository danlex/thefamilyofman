## Citation

Sontag, Susan. "Photographic Evangels." *The New York Review of Books* 23, no. 17 (4 November 1976).

Later revised and incorporated as Chapter 5 ("Photographic Evangels") of *On Photography* (New York: Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 1977). The chapter title in the book is identical to this essay's NYRB title. For the book compilation see `src-sontag-1977`.

## Relevance

The fourth of Sontag's five *New York Review of Books* essays. Addresses photography criticism and the discourse of photography's champions — those who made claims for photography as an autonomous art form and social instrument. This essay bears directly on the reception of *The Family of Man* as a paradigm case of photography instrumentalized for humanist-ideological purposes: Steichen as quintessential "photographic evangel." The essay's NYRB publication in late 1976 gave it currency in the photography-theory discourse just as *October* journal was being founded (1976) and the critical-theoretical moment was gathering momentum.

Tier 2: named-author critical theory of record. The chapter title's survival unchanged from NYRB to book makes this one of the most directly traceable essays to a specific book chapter.
