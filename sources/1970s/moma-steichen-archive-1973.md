## Relevance

The Edward Steichen Archive at MoMA is the primary institutional repository for documents relating to Steichen's curatorial career, including the period 1955–1973 most relevant to *The Family of Man* and its aftermath. The archive was assembled in the Department of Photography from 1968 onwards, with materials donated by Steichen, his family, friends, colleagues, and associates. After Steichen's death (25 March 1973), his wife Joanna Steichen donated the materials labelled "Steichen Bequest" between March and August 1973. This bequest constitutes the primary documentary record of Steichen's late career, including potential materials on the Luxembourg donation and his 1966 visit.
