## Relevance

This is the most significant academic event of the FoM 70th anniversary year (2025). The symposium was co-organised by three institutions: the C²DH (University of Luxembourg), the Centre national de l'audiovisuel (CNA, Steichen Collections), and the Trier Center for American Studies (TCAS, University of Trier). The programme engaged the primary active scholars of *The Family of Man* — Andreas Fickers, Claude Ewert, Emilia Sánchez González, Paul Lesch, Astrid Böger, and Shamoon Zamir — alongside Gilles Zeimet (then-CNA director) at Clervaux Castle itself.

The event marks the 70th anniversary of the exhibition's opening at MoMA (24 January 1955). It is the official academic recognition of the anniversary by the FoMLEG research consortium, and its programme signals the directions of current scholarly attention (local Luxembourg reception, heritagisation, global tour, decolonial perspectives).
