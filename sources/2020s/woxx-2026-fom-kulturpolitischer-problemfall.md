## Relevance

A pair of April 2026 *woxx* investigative articles on a conservation and governance crisis at the CNA Clervaux: specifically, a malfunction of the Clervaux Castle HVAC system that raised concerns about possible moisture/humidity damage to the *Family of Man* photographs. The articles cover:

1. The discovery (reported July 2025) that one of the two HVAC compressors at Clervaux Castle was defective and irreparable.
2. Culture Minister Eric Thill's statement that "climate values were constantly monitored and limit values never permanently exceeded, with no works damaged."
3. Parliamentary questioning by Déi Lénk MP Marc Baum on possible moisture damage to the UNESCO-listed photographs.
4. The resignation of CNA Director Gilles Zeimet (announced 24 April 2026), which the articles connect to this and other institutional crises (sexism allegations, a cancelled Teutloff exhibition, management problems).

This is the first conservation-incident source entry in the 2020s bibliography. The HVAC failure and its implications for the UNESCO-listed collection are directly relevant to the exhibition's current institutional status.
