She began shooting the project in 1978, at the age of 67, and continued until her death in 1997. The images – some 27,000 negatives – show private homes and their owners, ranging from countryside folk to the urban middle class."*

- **Direct museum-director quote (verbatim, body)**: *"'We have a good photography curriculum in the fine arts academies, strong artistically, historically and theoretically.
