## Key excerpts / pages

Verbatim content from page (fetched 2026-05-19):

- Birth/death given as 1918–2014 on this page (NOTE: discrepancy with ICP archive which gives 1917 — see notes below).
- LIFE career: began 1939; WWII coverage included Doolittle's raid on Tokyo, Guadalcanal landing, liberation of Paris.
- "the only civilian photographer present when the Germans surrendered to Eisenhower"
- Space program: 15 years documenting NASA; Mercury Seven dubbed him "Eighth Astronaut"
- Managing Editor George Hunt: "If LIFE could afford only one photographer, it would have to be Ralph Morse"
- No Family of Man connection mentioned on this page.
