## Relevance

A key German-language (bilingual) scholarly reappraisal of *The Family of Man*, edited by Jean Back (Luxembourg) and Viktoria Schmidt-Linsenhoff (University of Trier). The volume covers the exhibition from its 1955 creation through to 2001, encompassing the 1960s aftermath (USIA tour, Luxembourg donation), the postmodern critiques, and the UNESCO listing. Particularly relevant for the Luxembourg/European reception of the exhibition and for understanding the German-language scholarly conversation alongside the English-language scholarship of Sandeen (1995) and Stimson (2006).
