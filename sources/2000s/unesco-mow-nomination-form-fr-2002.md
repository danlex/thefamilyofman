## Relevance

The French-language nomination form is the parallel primary document to the English version (`src-unesco-mow-nomination-en-2002`). In the Luxembourg institutional context, French is the primary administrative language alongside Luxembourgish and German; the French form may therefore contain the most complete and formally authoritative statement of Luxembourg's argument to the IAC. It is of particular importance for CNA Luxembourg's own institutional history and for the francophone European reception of the exhibition.

For the same reasons as the English form (see `src-unesco-mow-nomination-en-2002`), this document is a priority primary source for `research/unesco.md` §3–4.
