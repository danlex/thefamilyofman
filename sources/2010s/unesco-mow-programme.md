## Relevance

The Memory of the World programme's own institutional pages — the framework under which *The Family of Man* was inscribed in 2003 (`src-unesco-mow-2003`). These three UNESCO pages anchor: programme founding date (1992); the International Advisory Committee structure; the inscription pathway (IAC recommendation + Executive Board endorsement); the 2015 UNESCO Recommendation that codified the programme; and the running total of inscribed items on the International Register (570 as of fetch). Required as a citable source any time the site or research notes describe the framework rather than the *Family of Man* register entry itself.

## Key excerpts / pages

Programme founding and aspiration (`/about`):

- The programme was established in **1992**, in response to growing concern about the deterioration and loss of documentary heritage worldwide.
- Programme aspiration: *"The world's documentary heritage belongs to all, should be fully preserved and protected for all and, with due recognition of cultural mores and practicalities, should be permanently accessible to all without hindrance."*

Governance structure (`/about`):

- The programme is governed at the international level by the **International Advisory Committee (IAC)**.
- The IAC comprises **14 members appointed by UNESCO's Director-General** and serves as the main advisory body.
- The programme operates through regional and national committees in addition to the IAC.

Normative framework (`/about`):

- The programme is normatively underpinned by the **2015 UNESCO Recommendation Concerning Preservation of, and Access to, Documentary Heritage** (including digital-born material).

Inscription pathway (`/register`):

- The International Register lists "documentary heritage which has been recommended by the International Advisory Committee, and endorsed by the Executive Board, as corresponding to the selection criteria regarding world significance and outstanding universal value."
- The two-step inscription pathway — IAC recommendation, Executive Board endorsement — is the formal pathway for every inscription on the register.
- The register has accepted inscriptions **since 1995**.

Aggregate scale (`/` overview):

- "**570 inscriptions on the International Register**" as of fetch 2026-04-29.
