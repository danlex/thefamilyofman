## Citation

Berger, John. *About Looking*. New York: Pantheon Books, 1980. 198 pp. ISBN 0-394-73655-7.

UK edition: London: Writers and Readers, 1980.

Essays collected from periodical publications of the 1970s, primarily written for *New Society* and other venues, 1970–1979.

## Relevance

The collection that most directly engages photography as a social and political practice within the tradition that *The Family of Man* represents. The essay "Uses of Photography" (written in response to Sontag's *On Photography*) is the most directly relevant: Berger accepts Sontag's critique of photography's political demobilizing function but proposes a counter-practice of politically motivated photographic archives. The essay "Paul Strand" and others address the documentary tradition that *Family of Man* drew from. *About Looking* thus represents both a critique of and an attempted recuperation of the humanist-documentary tradition.
