## Key excerpts / pages

Verbatim quotations from the page fetched 2026-05-06:

- Birth and death dates given as "1908–2004", nationality "French".
- Born in Chanteloup; studied literature at Cambridge University (1928–29).
- "He began photographing in 1931 and purchased a Leica in 1933."
- Joined an ethnographic expedition to Mexico in 1934.
- Studied cinematography with Paul Strand in 1935.
- Assisted filmmaker Jean Renoir (1936, 1939); created documentary *Return to Life* (1937).
- Drafted into French army's film and photo unit in 1940; taken prisoner by Germans that year; after three years of imprisonment, escaped and worked for the French underground.
- Photographed France's occupation and liberation (1944–1945).
- "In 1947 he co-founded the Magnum agency with Robert Capa, Chim (David Seymour), and George Rodger."
- Won Overseas Press Club Award four times; American Society of Magazine Photographers award (1953); Prix de la Société Française de Photographie (1959).
- Left Magnum in 1966 to pursue drawing.
