## Relevance

Steichen's only solo-show during his entire tenure as Director of the Department of Photography at MoMA. The exhibition opened after he had announced his retirement (confirmed via multiple secondary sources consulted this session), making it the institutional marker of the close of the Steichen era. Grace M. Mayer wrote the biographical outline; she was appointed Associate Curator in 1961 and subsequently Curator by Szarkowski in 1962.
