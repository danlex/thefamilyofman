## Relevance

Steichen's autobiography *A Life in Photography* (Doubleday / MoMA, 1963) was published in the year following his retirement from MoMA. As a major retrospective autobiography by the most prominent American photography curator of the postwar period, it would have received substantial coverage in the NYT Book Review and the cultural press. A NYT review of this book is a key data point for understanding how Steichen's self-presentation was received by the press establishment, and how the *Family of Man* was positioned in historical retrospect by Steichen himself.
