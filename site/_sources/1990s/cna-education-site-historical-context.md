## Relevance

The CNA's own educational platform historical-context page is a Tier-1 institutional source for the official institutional narrative of the *Family of Man* collection's transfer to Luxembourg, the pre-installation tour, and the June 1994 Clervaux inauguration. It provides the CNA's own account (in English and French) of the donation history (1964), Steichen's wish for Clervaux, the Toulouse/Tokyo/Hiroshima tour, and the permanent installation. The site was designed by Cropmark and is © 2021 CNA, confirming institutional authorship.
