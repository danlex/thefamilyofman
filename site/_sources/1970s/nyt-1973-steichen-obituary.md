## Relevance

The primary newspaper-of-record obituary for Edward Steichen, published the day after his death (25 March 1973). Steichen died at his farm in West Redding, Connecticut, two days before his 94th birthday. The NYT obituary is the standard secondary citation for Steichen's death in the scholarly literature on *The Family of Man*. Whitman's approach (he often pre-interviewed subjects before their deaths) may mean the obituary contains direct Steichen quotations not available elsewhere.
