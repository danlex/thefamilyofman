## Citation

Sontag, Susan. "Freak Show." *The New York Review of Books* 20, no. 18 (15 November 1973).

Later revised and incorporated into *On Photography* (New York: Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 1977). For the book compilation see `src-sontag-1977`.

## Relevance

The second of Sontag's five *New York Review of Books* essays. This essay is primarily focused on Diane Arbus and the aesthetic of the grotesque as a counter-tradition to the humanist photographic project exemplified by *The Family of Man*. By reading Arbus against the grain of Steichen's universalism, Sontag constructs a genealogy in which the sentimental humanism of the 1955 exhibition is the implicit target of critique. The essay matters for *Family of Man* reception as an articulation of the aesthetic-ideological opposition between 1950s humanist photography and the subsequent generation.
