## Relevance

Adams's review is one of a small cluster of named critical reviews of *The Family of Man* published in major US periodicals during the exhibition's opening run (January 24 – May 8, 1955). The title 'Through a Lens Darkly' signals a critical rather than celebratory framing — a counterpoint to the many enthusiastic press notices.
