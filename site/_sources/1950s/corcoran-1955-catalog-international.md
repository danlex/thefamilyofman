## Relevance

The Corcoran Gallery of Art (Washington D.C.) hosted the international edition of *The Family of Man* from June 30 to July 31, 1955 — the first stop of the international tour, nine days before the domestic U.S. tour opened in Minneapolis (June 21, 1955), and preceding Guatemala City by approximately two months. The Corcoran stop is documented in the MoMA press release (`src-moma-1955-press-release-book`, p. 2) as the international-edition opening. This Internet Archive item — the Corcoran's own copy of the catalog, digitised from their records — provides physical evidence of the international edition's distribution and is the earliest archival trace of the international tour's institutional footprint.
