## Relevance

This book review — of the *Family of Man* catalog published by Maco/MoMA — appears in October 1955, after the MoMA exhibition's close (May 8) and during the period of the catalog's mass commercial distribution. It represents the photography trade press's evaluation of the catalog as a publication in its own right, distinct from the March 1955 picture section that covered the exhibition. The October 1955 timing corresponds to the exhibition's first US tour stops (Chicago, Los Angeles, and other cities in the 1955–56 circuit).
