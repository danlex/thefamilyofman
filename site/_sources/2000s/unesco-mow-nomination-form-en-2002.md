## Tier justification

Tier 1: primary archival document — the formal nomination submission to the UNESCO Memory of the World Programme by the Government of Luxembourg, which constitutes the primary stated justification for the 2003 inscription. The submitting state, submission year (2002), and inscription year (2003) are confirmed from the UNESCO register page (freshly fetched 2026-04-30; `src-unesco-mow-2003`).

## Relevance

The English-language nomination form is the primary documentary source for the formal argument that Luxembourg presented to the UNESCO International Advisory Committee (IAC) in 2002. It would contain: (a) the formal description of the collection (potentially with figure counts different from or consistent with the inscribed "503 photographs, 273 photographers, 68 countries"); (b) the justification for "world significance and outstanding universal value"; (c) the conservation and access arrangements in place at CNA Clervaux; (d) endorsements or supporting letters from institutional partners; and (e) the formal statement of the nomination by the Luxembourg government.

This document is the strongest available primary source for the inscription's rationale and should be consulted for any future expansion of `research/unesco.md` §3 (The 2003 inscription) and §4 (Justification: what the register says).
