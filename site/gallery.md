      <h1>Gallery</h1>
      <p class="lead">A chronological reading of the exhibition's pre-history, making, touring, and afterlife — through photographs and installation views currently in this wiki under public-domain or Creative-Commons licences. Every plate of the 503 in the show remains under copyright with the photographers' estates and is not hosted here; the gallery surfaces only what is openly licensed, in the order it was taken.</p>
